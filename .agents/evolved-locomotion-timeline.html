evolved locomotionfull build plan, 19 days

genetic algorithm evolves neural net weights that drive a segmented creature's joints. quadruped, legs in pairs, from-scratch nn + ga, physics via library.

phase 1

day 4-5
small neural net, from scratch no libraries core depth

phase 2

evolution — the heart of the project

days 7-12

most days go here on purpose. this is what the judge said he wants to see. 4-leg genome means a bigger search space than 1-2 joints — extra buffer day built in.

day 7
genome + mutation + crossover low risk
genome = flat vector of net weights. mutation = gaussian noise at some rate. crossover = blend or split two parent genomes.
day 8
fitness function simple
horizontal distance traveled in a 5-10s trial window. planck gives position for free.
day 9-10
the generational loop headless, node highest risk
population ~30-50. run each genome's trial headless, no rendering. score fitness. truncation selection, keep top ~20%, breed the rest. repeat until fitness trends upward.
day 11
degenerate solution debugging near-certain
common failure: creatures learn to just topple forward and call it locomotion. fix with longer trials, penalize excessive rotation/falling, or require staying upright. budget this day specifically.
day 12
buffer — extra day for 4-leg complexity buffer
added versus the minimal-body plan. days 9-11 are the least predictable stretch of the build.
checkpoint — phase 2 · the project lives or dies here

fitness-over-generations curve trending up, even roughly. a specific generation you can point to where behavior visibly shifted from flailing to purposeful — quadrupedal gait emerging.

if this isn't real by day 12, don't move to phase 3 on hope.
